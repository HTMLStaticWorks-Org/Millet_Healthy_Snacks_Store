Crunchy Millet Chaat Bowl

Morning Millet Crunch

Top yoghurt or porridge with trail mix, seasonal fruit and a drizzle of honey for a joyful start.

The Feel-Good Snack Board

Arrange millet puffs, fresh fruit, nuts, dips and crisp veggies for an easy gathering platter.

Mix dates, cocoa, oats and crushed nuts. Roll into bite-sized treats and chill until firm.
