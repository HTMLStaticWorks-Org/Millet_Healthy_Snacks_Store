The Grain Breakdown

Meet the 5 Super Millets & Their Superpowers

Each native variety brings an extraordinary micronutrient profile that modern cereal crops simply cannot match.

FINGER MILLET (RAGI)

The Calcium King

Contains 344 mg of Calcium per 100g — more than triple the calcium in milk. Fortifies bone density, supports growing teeth, and is essential for maternal nourishment.

✓ High Polyphenols • Zero Gluten
SORGHUM (JOWAR)

The Gut Microbiome Booster

Packed with resistant starch and beta-glucan fibers that ferment slowly in the large intestine, feeding beneficial bifidobacteria and preventing acid reflux.

✓ Low Glycemic Load • High Prebiotic
PEARL MILLET (BAJRA)

The Iron & Stamina Dynamo

Remarkably high in organic bio-available Iron (8 mg/100g) and Zinc. Combats chronic fatigue, prevents anemia, and promotes radiant oxygenated skin.

✓ Iron Dynamo • Magnesium Rich
FOXTAIL MILLET (KANGNI)

Blood Sugar & Satiety Champion

Has one of the lowest glycemic indexes among all grains (GI: 50.8). Slowly trickles complex carbohydrates into the bloodstream, eradicating sugar crashes.

✓ Diabetic Friendly • Steady Energy
LITTLE MILLET (SAMAI)

Cardiovascular Guardian

Abundant in natural B-vitamins, niacin, and phytosterols. Clinically documented to help balance serum cholesterol levels and support arterial flexibility.

✓ Heart Tonic • Antioxidant Rich
CLIMATE BONUS

Gentle on the Earth

Millets thrive in arid soils with only rain-fed moisture. By eating millets, you conserve precious groundwater and enrich organic soil biodiversity.

✓ 70% Less Water • Carbon Negative
