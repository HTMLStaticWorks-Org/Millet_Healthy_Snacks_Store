🌾 Roots & Purpose

Good Food Has a Story

Ours begins with a deep reverence for India's native grains, direct fair-trade partnerships with smallholder farmers, and an unwavering belief: daily snacking should be full of vitality, flavor, and integrity.

Our Mission

Bringing the Magic of Millets to Every Home

Millets nourished ancient civilizations across the Indian subcontinent for thousands of years. As modern agricultural subsidies favored water-thirsty wheat and rice, these resilient supergrains became forgotten treasures.

At Millet&More, we are restoring millets to their rightful place at the center of the Indian pantry. Through air-convection roasting and beloved culinary seasonings, we make ancient nutrition effortlessly delicious.
