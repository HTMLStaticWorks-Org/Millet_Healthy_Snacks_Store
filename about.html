A little about us

Good food has a story.

Ours begins with a love for India's grains, a curiosity for flavour and a simple belief: everyday snacking can feel a little more thoughtful.

Our why

Bringing the goodness of millets to more moments.

Millets have nourished communities for generations. At Millet&More, we're inspired by their versatility and by the people who grow, cook and share them.

We create approachable snacks that make it easy to explore these grains in new ways — with familiar flavours, satisfying textures and a whole lot of care.
