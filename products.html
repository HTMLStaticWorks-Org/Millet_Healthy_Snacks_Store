What Goes In & What Stays Out

We read labels so you don't have to worry. Our clean pantry promise ensures pure nourishment.

✓ Always What Goes In

  • 100% Native Whole Millets: Sprouted Ragi, Pearl Millet (Bajra), Sorghum (Jowar), and Foxtail.
  • Cold-Pressed Virgin Oils: Wood-pressed groundnut & sesame oils rich in natural antioxidants.
  • Sun-Dried Natural Spices: Whole cumin, black pepper, Himalayan pink salt, and fresh curry leaves.
  • Unrefined Sweeteners: Wild forest honey and organic palm jaggery, never white chemical sugar.

✕ Never What Stays Out

  • Zero Palm Oil: Never hydrogenated oils, trans fats, or cheap refined palm blends.
  • Zero Refined Flour (Maida): We never dilute our whole grains with filler flours.
  • Zero Synthetic Preservatives: No BHA, BHT, sodium benzoate, or artificial colorants.
  • Zero Chemical Flavor Boosters: Absolutely no monosodium glutamate (MSG) or artificial smoke powders.
